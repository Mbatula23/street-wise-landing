

# Murphy Street Partners — Professional Landing Page

## Overview
A clean, prestigious single-page landing site for Murphy Street Partners, inspired by the refined aesthetics of top-tier financial institutions like JP Morgan and Goldman Sachs. Dark, elegant colour scheme with a polished, minimal layout.

---

## Design Direction
- **Colour palette:** Deep navy/charcoal background, crisp white text, subtle gold accents for key elements (dividers, logo highlight, hover states)
- **Typography:** Elegant serif font for the company name, clean sans-serif for body — conveying trust and authority
- **Feel:** Minimal, spacious, confident — no clutter, every element intentional

---

## Page Structure

### 1. Navigation Bar
- Murphy Street Partners logo (top-left)
- Single "Enquire" link (top-right) that opens `mailto:contact@murphy-street.com`
- Slim, semi-transparent bar that sits over the hero

### 2. Hero Section
- Full-viewport dark background with a subtle gradient or understated texture (no moving image)
- Company name **"Murphy Street Partners"** large and centred
- Short tagline beneath: **"Strategic Sports Advisory"** (or similar — editable)
- A thin gold divider line between name and tagline
- "Enquire" call-to-action button with a refined outlined style

### 3. Footer
- Company address: 19 Grosvenor Gardens, Belgravia, London, SW1W 0BD
- Copyright line
- Privacy Policy link
- Subtle, minimal styling consistent with the dark theme

---

## Key Details
- **Fully responsive** — looks great on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **No backend needed** — purely static landing page
- **Contact action** opens an email to `contact@murphy-street.com` with subject "Enquiry"
- **Smooth, subtle animations** on load (fade-in for text elements) to add polish without being flashy
